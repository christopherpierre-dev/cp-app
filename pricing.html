Questions

What's the difference between Pro and Institution?

Pro is for one person having 1-to-1 conversations (Two-Way Call). Institution is for hosting multilingual meetings and conferences with several participants — priced by the hour or by seat.

What does “unlimited, fair use” mean on Pro?

Everyday back-and-forth conversations are unlimited. Very heavy, continuous use (many hours a day) may be paced to keep the service fast for everyone — it suits personal use, not running events.

How does billing work?

Payments are handled securely by Lemon Squeezy, our merchant of record. Applicable sales tax / VAT is calculated and included automatically for your country.

Do I pay through the App Store?

No. You subscribe here on the web and sign in to the app with your account, so there are no app-store surcharges.
